# Sample Workflow (illustrative, fictional)

1. A fictional foundation, *Example Foundation*, is discovered.
2. Research notes a stated focus on youth education (fictional).
3. A grant opportunity is extracted and the prospect is ranked high.
4. A personalised email is drafted and held for approval.
5. The owner edits one sentence and approves.
6. The email is sent and logged.
7. If a reply arrives it is recorded; otherwise a follow-up is proposed for approval.

Fictional example. It does not represent real organisations or outcomes.
