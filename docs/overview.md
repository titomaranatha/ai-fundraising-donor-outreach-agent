# Overview

## Fundraising problem
Funding for NGOs and social ventures depends on finding the right sponsors and approaching them well. Prospecting is slow, and funders' priorities differ. Generic outreach performs poorly and can damage reputation.

## Purpose
Automate research and drafting while a human makes every decision to contact a prospect.

## Intended users
NGO founders, fundraising leads, and small teams seeking sponsorship, grants, or CSR partnerships.

## Honest scope
This repository documents a workflow design. It does not state that any funding was obtained or that any organisation replied. Such claims require records.
