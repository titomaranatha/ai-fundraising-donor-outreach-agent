# Security & Responsible Outreach

- **Explicit approval** is required before any outreach email is sent.
- **No donor or prospect personal information** is stored in this repository. `.gitignore` excludes data folders.
- **No private email conversations** or confidential organisational data are published.
- **No credentials** are committed; use placeholders.
- **Truthfulness:** do not claim funding, endorsements, or responses without records.
- **Responsible outreach:** target relevant funders only, avoid mass blasts, honour opt-outs, and follow applicable data-protection and email laws.
- **Data minimisation:** collect only what is needed to contact a funder about a relevant opportunity.
