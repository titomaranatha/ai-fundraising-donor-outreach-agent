# Workflow

1. **Discover** potential donors, NGOs, foundations, philanthropists, CSR opportunities.
2. **Identify** organisations that support relevant causes.
3. **Research** potential sponsors and their funding priorities.
4. **Extract** relevant sponsorship and grant opportunities.
5. **Qualify and prioritise** prospects.
6. **Generate** personalised sponsorship and fundraising emails.
7. **Review and prepare** emails for approval.
8. **Approval:** the owner explicitly approves before any send.
9. **Track** sent communications and responses.
10. **Follow up** and maintain outreach records.

## Prospect qualification
Qualification weighs cause alignment, evidence of an active funding programme, fit with the opportunity, and contactability. The exact scoring rules are **unverified**.

## Personalised emails
Emails reference the prospect's stated priorities from research. They must not invent relationships, past support, or outcomes.

## Human-in-the-loop approval
- Drafts are held in a pending state.
- Only the owner can approve.
- Edited drafts are re-reviewed.
- Rejected drafts are not sent.

## Follow-up and tracking
Sent messages and responses are recorded. Follow-ups are proposed from those records and also need approval before sending (design intent; mechanism unverified).
